The Origin

Born in the
Still Waters.

Before it was a snack, it was a seed. Harvested from the Euryale ferox lily in the ponds of Bihar, India, our seeds are hand-picked by farming communities that have cultivated them for centuries.

No machinery, no chemicals. Just sun, water, and tradition.

Meet the Founder Follow the Journey

A Note from the Founder

Sarah Thompson

I discovered makhana during a trip to Bihar, India. Watching farmers hand-pop these ancient seeds over open fires, I knew I had to share this tradition with the world. Rooted exists to bring you snacks that honor the earth, your body, and the people who grow them. Every bag tells a story of heritage, sustainability, and nourishment. Thank you for being part of this journey with us.

— Sarah
